📅 02/03/2026 – 09/03/2026

This was my 6th week as a Smart Contract Auditor Intern at Bybit.

This week I completed the security report on the Chainlink CCIP protocol, another cross-chain bridge solution. So far, I’ve studied Hyperlane, LayerZero, and CCIP.

Honestly, I find these three quite similar in their design and architecture. However, CCIP focuses more on risk management. While LayerZero allows many customizable components, CCIP turns many of these elements into managed services operated by the CCIP team and includes additional compliance and safety mechanisms.

I think I’ve now covered most of the popular cross-chain protocols. It’s been really exciting to learn about these systems, and I feel that I’m improving quickly.
